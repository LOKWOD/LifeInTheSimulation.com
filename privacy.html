PRIVACY

Privacy matters here.

This site is designed to work without invasive tracking. If analytics, newsletter delivery, comments or other services are added later, this page will be updated to explain what data is collected and why.

Newsletter

The current newsletter form is a visual preview and does not transmit or store your email address. A production mailing service will be connected before subscriptions are accepted.

External services

Links to third-party sites are governed by their own privacy practices. Life in the Simulation does not control those services.

Editorial principle

A site about attention and digital systems should not quietly undermine its own message. Data collection will be kept to the minimum needed to operate the publication.
