PRIVACY

LAST UPDATED / AUGUST 14, 2026

A quiet site should not need to watch everything you do.

This static publication is designed to work without accounts, ad trackers or behavioral profiling.

AT A GLANCE

  • No user accounts
  • No advertising trackers
  • No analytics scripts
  • No data-selling
  • No newsletter form
  • Theme preference stays in your browser

Information this site collects

Life in the Simulation does not intentionally collect personal information through the website. There are no account registrations, comment forms, advertising pixels or embedded analytics services.

Local browser storage

If you change the color theme, your browser may store that preference locally so the site can remember it on your next visit. The preference is not transmitted to the site owner.

Server and hosting logs

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and delivered through internet infrastructure that may create ordinary technical logs, such as request time, requested page, browser information and network address. Those systems operate under their own policies.

External links

Links to external websites leave this domain. Those sites may use cookies, analytics or other data practices that are outside the control of Life in the Simulation.

RSS

The RSS feed is a static file. Reading it does not create an account with this site. Your chosen RSS application may have its own privacy practices.

Changes

This page will be updated if the site adds forms, analytics, advertising or another feature that changes how data is handled.
