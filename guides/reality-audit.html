FIELD GUIDE 001 • 30 MINUTES

Run a Reality Audit

Use this when the world feels unusually noisy, threatening, urgent or performative. The goal is not to decide what is “real” in a cosmic sense. It is to identify what you directly know versus what has been mediated.

01 / CLOSE THE FEEDS

For ten minutes, stop incoming information. No social feeds, news, email, markets or notifications.

02 / NAME FIVE DIRECT FACTS

Write five things you know through immediate observation: where you are, what your body feels, who is physically nearby, what work is actually in front of you.

03 / NAME FIVE MEDIATED FACTS

Write five things currently occupying your mind that arrived through a screen, another person, a metric or a headline.

04 / CHECK THE SOURCE CHAIN

For each mediated item, ask: primary source, reliable secondary source, anonymous claim, prediction, opinion, or algorithmically selected anecdote?

05 / FIND THE ACTION

Can you do anything useful about it today? If yes, define one action. If no, decide whether continued attention is buying you anything.

06 / RETURN TO PHYSICAL REALITY

Spend five minutes doing something tangible: walk outside, wash a dish, inspect a project, talk face-to-face, or simply look around without recording it.

The result

You are not trying to become uninformed. You are restoring scale. Your nervous system was not designed to experience every distant event as if it were happening in the room.
