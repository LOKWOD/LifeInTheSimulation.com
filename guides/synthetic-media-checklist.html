FIELD GUIDE 003 • 5 MINUTES

Synthetic Media Check

Use this before forwarding or acting on a surprising image, video, audio clip or screenshot—especially when the claim is consequential or emotionally loaded.

01 / FIND THE ORIGINAL

Do not verify a repost when you can find the first publication, full clip, original document or official statement.

02 / CHECK THE ACCOUNT

Is the publisher identifiable? Does the account have a credible history? Was it created yesterday? Does it routinely post fabricated material?

03 / SEEK INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION

For important claims, look for more than multiple accounts repeating the same source. Find genuinely independent reporting or primary evidence.

04 / INSPECT CONTEXT

Check date, location and the material before and after the clip. Real footage is often misrepresented without needing any AI at all.

05 / NOTICE THE URGENCY

“Share before they delete this” is not evidence. Pressure to react before verifying is itself a reason to slow down.

Do not over-rely on artifacts

Odd fingers, strange reflections and unnatural speech can be clues, but generators improve and compression creates its own artifacts. Provenance and corroboration are stronger than visual guessing.
