FIELD GUIDE 007PRACTICE

The Direct Experience Week

No digital detox. Just one deliberately unmediated experience each day.

7 days 3 min read Updated Aug 14, 2026 Ready to run By Life in the Simulation Editorial Desk

PROTOCOL MAP

No digital detox. Just one deliberately unmediated experience each day.

This field guide is a counterweight, not a rejection of technology. Complete one exercise per day. Keep it private unless sharing is part of the experience.

Time20–60 minutes daily
RuleNo recording during the exercise
GoalRestore sensory and social depth

Day 1: navigate from memory

Travel a familiar route without turn-by-turn directions. Notice landmarks, orientation and what your mental map has retained.

Day 2: handle the material

Repair, cook, plant, clean, draw or build something with a visible physical result. Let the object's resistance teach you.

Day 3: have a whole-room conversation

Spend at least twenty minutes with another person and every device out of reach. Do not photograph the meeting.

Day 4: observe one place

Sit outdoors or near a public window for thirty minutes. Record nothing. Track sound, temperature, movement and changes in light.

NOTICE

What became visible only after the first ten minutes?

Day 5: use a complete source

Read a full chapter, watch a full lecture or inspect a complete document instead of clips and summaries. Note what context changed the conclusion.

Day 6: make an unscored effort

Exercise without tracking, read without logging, create without posting or help without documenting. Let the activity end without a metric.

Day 7: choose a recurring reality anchor

Select one practice to repeat weekly: a local walk, shared meal, workshop hour, market visit, game, garden task or conversation.

After the week

Write three sentences:

  • The exercise that felt most difficult was…
  • The experience that felt richer than expected was…
  • The part of direct life I will protect is…

Direct experience is not automatically wiser than mediated experience. Its value is that it gives your models something real to answer to.

END OF FIELD GUIDE 007

Run it once before improving it.

The purpose of a protocol is changed behavior, not a perfect-looking plan. Record what happened, keep the useful part and discard the theater.

Make the week measurable without turning it into a score

The experiment works when it increases contact with the world, not when it produces a perfect streak. Choose one direct activity each day and write a two-sentence observation afterward.

  • Name the physical place, person or object you will attend to.
  • Set a clear beginning and end so the exercise remains practical.
  • Keep capture tools out of reach unless they are essential to the activity.
  • At the end, note one detail you would probably have missed through a screen.

Review the observations after seven days. Keep only the practices that changed what you noticed or remembered.
