FIELD GUIDE 002 • 7 DAYS

The Attention Reset

You do not need to become anti-technology. You need to make attention something you allocate instead of something interfaces automatically harvest.

DAY 1 / REMOVE PUSH

Disable every non-human notification that does not protect money, safety, work obligations or an actual appointment.

DAY 2 / MOVE THE BAIT

Take social and news apps off your first home screen. Keep access, add friction.

DAY 3 / CHOOSE INPUTS

Bookmark a small set of sources you intentionally visit. For one day, use those instead of algorithmic discovery.

DAY 4 / CREATE AN EMPTY HOUR

Spend one hour without simultaneous media. Walk, drive, work, cook or sit without filling the background.

DAY 5 / SINGLE-TASK

Pick one meaningful task and work on it for 45 uninterrupted minutes. Put the phone physically out of reach.

DAY 6 / REPLACE, DON'T JUST REMOVE

Use the reclaimed time for something you actually value: a person, project, book, workout, workshop or outdoor place.

DAY 7 / WRITE YOUR DEFAULTS

Decide what stays. Attention systems work through defaults; create your own before they create them for you.

A good reset is boring

No dramatic digital detox is required. Small persistent friction changes behavior more reliably than a weekend declaration followed by the same old defaults.
