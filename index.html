THE PREMISE

You don’t have to believe we live in a simulation to notice how much of life is already simulated.

Money is numbers on screens. Reputation is metrics. Friendship is partially mediated by feeds. Memory lives in clouds. Navigation is outsourced to satellites. Algorithms decide what we see, what we buy and increasingly what we think deserves attention.

Life in the Simulation explores the line between reality and representation—without pretending certainty where none exists. The goal is not escape. It is clarity.
