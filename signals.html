SHORT TRANSMISSIONS

Signals worth noticing.

Brief observations from the border between direct life and mediated life.

SIGNAL 001

The map is not the territory.

Your feed is a model of the world selected for engagement. Treat it as an instrument panel, not a window.

Attention
SIGNAL 002

Friction can be freedom.

Convenience removes tiny moments where you used to decide. Adding a little friction can put choice back into the loop.

Design
SIGNAL 003

Proof is becoming a product.

When images, voices and documents can be synthesized cheaply, authenticated provenance becomes more valuable.

AI
SIGNAL 004

Metrics are compression.

A score can summarize a thing. It cannot become the thing without losing information.

Systems
SIGNAL 005

Boredom is unclaimed compute.

The empty moments that platforms rush to fill are often where memory, imagination and self-directed thought resume.

Mind
SIGNAL 006

Direct experience has no share button.

Some of the best evidence that you were alive will never become content.

Practice
